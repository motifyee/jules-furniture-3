Our Story

Three generations of wood dust and uncompromising standards.

Damietta isn't just where we work. It is the undisputed furniture capital of Egypt, recognized globally for woodworking excellence.

Founded by Master Carpenter Ahmed El-Sayed in 1978, our workshop began with a simple premise: build furniture that outlasts its owner. Today, while we've adopted modern precision tools, the core remains unchanged. Every piece is hand-finished. Every joint is inspected by human eyes. We don't cut corners because in solid wood, there is nowhere to hide them.
